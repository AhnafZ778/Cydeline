Record actual recruitment, consent, accommodations and attendance locally. Avoid collecting real company credentials or sensitive incident data. No participant results exist in this record.

## Sessions and counterbalancing

Use comparable validated variants. Four beginners follow order A→B→C and four C→B→A; rotate scenarios between cohorts. Record assignment before observing performance. Difficulty stays fixed at launch. Save run IDs, manifest hashes, seed, model configuration, traces, assistance level and evidence revisions. Record failures and deviations, including model outages and facilitator help.

1. Independent baseline: explain the public company brief and rubric; observe without helping investigate.
2. Coached investigation: allow Explain/Nudge/Guide and reference exercises. Capture which help was requested, why and whether it changed reasoning.
3. Unfamiliar independent variation: disable coaching; ask the learner to explain evidence pivots and containment tradeoffs.
4. Approximately one week later: another independent variation to examine retention. Record the actual interval rather than calling it one week automatically.

Experts separately review realism, public escalation policy, accepted alternative reasoning and the rubric. Keep expert findings separate from learner results. A model-only or synthetic session is engineering evidence, never a participant session.

## Human scoring, separate from the server's exercise score

Score each skill 0–2. 0 = absent or materially incorrect; 1 = partially supported reasoning with a meaningful unresolved gap; 2 = defensible reasoning linked to observations and appropriate uncertainty. Do not grade exact prose or a prescribed query order. Record evidence for every score and reviewer disagreement.

| Skill | Example observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Triage | States role, observed concern and policy-based escalation reason |
| Querying | Makes two meaningful evidence pivots to test a question |
| Evidence interpretation | Distinguishes observed values, hypotheses and contradicting evidence |
| Scoping | Identifies observed access paths and remaining unknowns |
| Containment judgment | Explains scope and measures preservation of legitimate work |
| Recovery and handoff | Uses fresh independent checks and communicates unresolved uncertainty |

NICE mapping remains an expert review task; do not imply NICE certification or professional readiness.

## Observation sheet

For each participant use a pseudonymous ID and record background band, assigned order, session dates, scenario/variant/run IDs, assistance history, facilitator interventions, hesitations/misconceptions, two pivots, containment outcome, workflow availability, six scores with evidence, saved-work incidents, and feedback disputes. Record independently scored baseline/transfer/follow-up results before comparing them. Preserve negative findings.

## Progression decision

- At least six of eight make two meaningful evidence pivots without facilitator help.
- At least six contain the unfamiliar variation while preserving legitimate workflows.
- Median independent performance improves at least two points on the twelve-point human rubric.
- No participant loses acknowledged saved work.
- Experts identify no unresolved critical realism or grading defect.

Mark each gate pass/fail/unavailable with participant counts and exact evidence. Missing or excluded sessions remain visible. These formative gates are not statistically conclusive evidence of effectiveness. Revise the learning flow when they fail. Schedule invitations and contact participants only after explicit human authorization; this document does not send messages.
